# MockMentor User Guide

## 1. Create an account
Open the app and go to **/login**. Click **New here? Create an account**, enter your email and a password of at least 6 characters, and click **Sign up**. Next time, use **Log in**.

## 2. Start an interview
On **New interview**, choose a **role** and a **level**, then click **Start interview**. The button stays disabled until you pick both. Starting takes a few seconds while the first question is generated.

## 3. Answer questions
Each interview has 6 questions. Read the question card (it shows the difficulty), type your answer in the box (up to 2,000 characters), and click **Submit answer**. Analysis takes roughly 5-20 seconds.

## 4. Read your feedback
After each answer you see a score out of 10, your strengths, and what to improve. Click **Next question** to continue. The next question adapts: strong answers get harder questions, weak answers get easier ones.

## 5. Finish
After question 6, click **Finish and see summary** to see your overall score, a short summary, and your top 3 areas to improve.

## 6. Review history
Open **/history** to see all your interviews with date, role, level, and score. Click a finished one to reread its summary, or an unfinished one to continue it.

## Tips
- Write complete answers with examples. Short answers score low.
- If you refresh the page mid-interview, you return to your current question.
- If you see an error, wait a moment and try again. The AI service can be briefly busy.
